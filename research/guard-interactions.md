# Compose local guard consequences before expanding another branch

24 November 2026. Checked guard consequences refute 61 of the 648 previously
recorded local residuals. Twenty require binary guards beyond the unit-guard
baseline. Three local roots were absent from the earlier budget-limited query's
retained history. These are new source-bound compositions, not new completed
whole inputs. The code remains an offline discovery and proof experiment.

## What can be reused exactly

A checked unit refutation of support H under guard G establishes the clause
negating G. At a recorded local residual C, we first restrict the support by the
fixed source assignment and transport its coordinates. Every surviving clause
must occur in C. The remaining guard is then negated to form a local consequence
D, and the existing exact-core RUP checker verifies C together with not-D is
unit-refutable. Deleted support, incompatible assignments and missing local
clauses cannot supply a hint. Twelve compatible supports fail local containment
in this census; all misses remain recorded.

Let Q contain the admitted consequences. Then C entails Q, and the models of C
equal the models of C and Q together. Q alone is a necessary condition. Its
satisfying assignments do not replace the original clauses. All assignments
excluded by Q have checked reasons; the region satisfying Q remains subject to
C. This is an exact description with an unresolved region, not an efficiently
closed partition of arbitrary SAT. Clause learning, RUP checking and implication
graphs are established techniques; this experiment connects existing repository
implementations to the saved conditional supports.

The census records every guard width, then compares three binary fragments:
original clauses of width at most two, guard consequences of width at most two,
and their union. A path from a literal to its negation refutes that premise.
Opposite paths for one variable refute the fragment. These graph claims are
checked separately from source-level publication. Wider guards remain explicit
constraints outside this binary closure.

The native compiler turns a path into an actual source-cofactor refutation.
For each edge, its original clause or supported guard template refutes the
branch that violates that implication. The other branch follows the path.
Both branches use the existing native split rule; leaves use checked unit
certificates. Two opposite paths and one final split refute the entire local C.
No fresh SAT search or new native proof rule is introduced. A conservative trace
transport miss uses the existing small-support materializer, with its propagation
charged. The source CNF, not just Q, appears in the published native certificate.

## Costs: prefer full local closure over isolated forced values

The frozen first phase selects at most two candidate literals per context and
sixteen per target, prioritizing mixed witnesses and then literal order. It
compares 116 source cofactors from empty stores. Every selected witness uses a
new binary guard clause. One cold acquisition pair per cofactor is diagnostic;
it is not a statistically established speed comparison.

| Target operation | Compiled acquisition | Fresh acquisition | Compiled / fresh rows |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 116 individual exclusions | 1.64082 s | 1.50819 s | 740 / 545 |
| 61 complete local refutations | 0.80095 s | 1.26243 s | 413 / 470 |

Isolated exclusion compilation costs 8.79% more time and stores more proof
nodes. The adaptive second phase, explicitly frozen after the census, takes
every contradictory context and compiles both paths of its smallest contradictory
variable. This local acquisition costs 36.55% less time and 12.13% fewer rows.
All fresh controls also complete within their individual 256-dispatch allowance.
The difference therefore concerns local acquisition cost, not a newly decidable
case. Provisioned supports and discovery are additional obligations.

Three local roots, all on edit 28 at events 78--80, are absent after the original
whole query exhausts its shared 256-dispatch budget. The first two need binary
guards; the third does not. They now have native refutations. A fresh individual
budget can also refute them, and the experiment does not integrate these closures
into the original query. No whole-input completion or live speedup is established.

Discovery and checked transport take 7.32573 seconds; hint persistence adds
7.94632 seconds. The first complete study takes 37.59133 seconds, including
selected acquisitions, serialization, compression, 6.50283 seconds of warm
replay and 7.60178 seconds of cold replay. Its process peak RSS is 348,749,824
bytes. The closure follow-up takes 5.29129 seconds including restoration,
comparators, checks, preservation and compression. Original acquisition/capture
and the final historical audit remain outside these study times. These totals
must accompany the smaller acquisition-only numbers.

The exact-core hint bank occupies 117,690,368 SQLite bytes for 67,898,593 logical
key/payload bytes, compressed to 2,751,615 bytes. It repeats full core identities
and bodies across 6,100 hints but has only 616 distinct cores. Sharing those core
bodies is a concrete storage opportunity; this experiment measures the existing
representation and does not claim an implemented memory reduction. The native
selected and closure banks occupy 15,339,520 and 10,805,248 SQLite bytes. They
include both comparison arms and are not single-query live-memory requirements.

## Bounds and next target

For V graph variables and E implication edges, this census runs up to 2V
reachability searches, costing O(V(V+E)) time with linear scratch state per
search, plus retained witness paths. A live contradiction-only test needs one
SCC pass and two paths, rather than enumerating every forced value. Preparation,
source/support matching and proof checking remain additional. The current
prototype repeatedly validates templates and canonical cores; its census cost
must not be treated as the cost of a future shared validation scheme.

A path of k edges compiles to O(k) unit/split nodes before exact-key reuse.
Full source-cofactor bodies and child keys can require O(kL) literal slots for an
L-literal source, or O(kL log(N+1)) bits for N source variables, with repeated
restriction, normalization and verification.
This explains why a short graph path need not yield a cheaper native certificate.
The 256-edge and admission limits bound selected output, not all discovery,
verification, retained banks or process memory. Obtaining enough useful guards
for every CNF has no established uniform polynomial bound.

The first next target is a live, selective local-contradiction gate. Start with
opposite unit guards, then units plus original binary clauses, and only then
supported binary guards where useful. Transport only active short guards;
preserve wider constraints without eagerly materializing all of them. Compare
whole-query cost and completion with ordinary search and the existing guided
policy, including misses, repeated structure and cold replay. Do not replace
the original input with a satisfiable consequence graph. Core sharing in the
hint store is second; automatic origin discovery and fresh evaluation remain
open.
