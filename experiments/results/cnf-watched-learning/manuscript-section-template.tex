\subsection{Watched propagation with complete active knowledge}
\label{sec:watched-learning}

The bounded-admission trial reduced propagation cost but lost two of the three
checked answers.  The next experiment keeps every surviving clause active and
changes the partial-assignment index.  The old occurrence engine updates each
incident clause on every assignment and undo.  The candidate uses two watched
literals to discover units and conflicts.  Complete-proposal satisfaction counts
update only when a committed value differs from its proposal value, with
symmetric undo.  Known list-length charges are accumulated in bulk.  This is an
implementation bundle, not an isolated timing attribution to watch movement.
No production default, admission rule or proof format changes.

A newly false watched literal triggers its watch list.  If the other watch is
true, processing can stop; otherwise the watch moves to a non-false replacement
when one exists.  With no replacement, the clause enters a candidate set.
Initially, unit and empty clauses enter that set.  Each candidate is checked
against the current partial assignment, and stale candidates are discarded.
The smallest actual unit/conflict clause index is processed, preserving the old
order rather than replacing it with FIFO propagation.  New learned clauses watch
their highest-level literals, including the asserting literal.  The asserting
clause is queued after the backjump; watch positions themselves are not undone.

\begin{proposition}[Trace preservation under an exact propagation frontier]
Suppose two runs begin with the same canonical clauses and complete proposal,
use the same decision, conflict-analysis and backjump rules, and process the
least-indexed actual unit or conflicting clause at each propagation step.
Replacing full occurrence maintenance by a watched candidate index that never
omits an actual unit or conflict, with stale entries validated before use,
preserves the decisions, resolution records and budgeted result.  Complete-model
counts may omit assignment and undo updates whose values equal the proposal.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Induct on propagation and decision steps.  Both runs have the same fixed values,
so validating a candidate gives the same unit, conflict or stale classification.
Completeness and the minimum-index rule therefore select the same actual clause.
A unit fixes the same literal with the same reason.  A conflict has the same
trail and reasons, giving identical resolution records and the same backjump.
At a propagation-closed decision point the complete model, activity and gain
values coincide, so the same variable and phase are chosen.  If a value equals
the proposal it does not change the complete model, so omitting its count update
and undo preserves that model's clause-satisfaction counts.  The induction also
preserves the entered-context count and hence the budgeted outcome.
\end{proof}

The watch invariant supplies frontier completeness.  An unsatisfied clause
cannot lose its second non-false watched literal without a notification: the
notified watch either finds another non-false literal or queues the remaining
unit/conflict.  Backtracking restores a previously propagation-closed prefix.
An older learned clause either retains its implication or regains at least two
free literals when moving below its asserting level; the newly asserting clause
is queued explicitly.  Retaining watches after undo therefore does not require
scanning every incident clause.  Validation separately compares full output
proofs and diagnostics, rather than inferring trace equality from matching SAT
answers alone.  The unchanged resolution checker remains the authority for
all target conclusions.

WATCHED_MEASUREMENTS

The same sixteen exposed edited targets and 2,000-context cap are used.  All
retained clauses remain active and the full retained proof prefix is copied,
checked and persisted.  Scientific source hashes and two alternating run orders
are frozen before measured outcomes.  The finite exposed panel is not fresh
external transfer, and the sixteen other edits of SAT parents are not added to
its denominator.  General recognition, preprocessing and unrestricted expansion
obligations remain explicit.

Measured cost includes input/hash reads, parent reads and checks, transport,
augmentation, watch construction and updates, search, proof substitution,
checking, writes, three compressions and replay.  Historical acquisition,
imports/startup, reports and independent audits are additional.  Two rounds
labelled cold in the data parse fresh objects in the same worker after its warm
round; they are not independent cold-start process timings and do not flush OS
caches.  Separate fresh workers reproduce all answers and checker counters with
search, expansion and transport disabled, outside benchmark costs.

All 2,679 full and 20 focused tests pass.  A reproduced audit covers all 512
two-variable clause sets/all seeds, 3,304 three-variable clause sets of at most
three clauses/two seeds, and 256 sparse signed duplicate/tautological mixtures
with some units/two seeds.  Budgets 0, 2 and 20,000 yield 27,414 exact proof and
diagnostic comparisons: 11,689 SAT, 5,576 UNSAT and 10,149 unknown.  Independent
truth tables check every completed answer and 3,971 learned-clause occurrences.
The audit exercises 19,394 watch moves, 4,533 stale candidates, 3,426 duplicate
learning encounters and six nonchronological jumps.  An additional 1,024 composed
trace comparisons cover source deletion and shared-variable additions, retaining
the complete old proof prefix.  Repeated seeds, budgets and edits are not
independent samples.  An initial focused fixture did not exercise its intended
satisfied-watch branch; its failure log is preserved and the fixture is corrected
without changing search code.

Validation preserves all 252 earlier manifests, 84,641 artifact entries,
1,372 scientific sources and 91 previous proofs.  Five scientific files and one
proof are added.  The next coverage question is whether a separately frozen
expansion budget can turn saved work into answers on the thirteen remaining
edited targets.  Fresh external transfer, proof retention and alternative
supports after source deletion remain open.  Equal-trace speedups do not define
a larger tractable SAT class or prove an end-to-end amortization bound.
