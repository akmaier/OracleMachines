\subsection{Selective survival and exact composition after source edits}
\label{sec:selective-learning}

A deleted source premise need not invalidate every learned consequence.  The
previous whole-collection guard rejected an entire proof whenever one referenced
original clause disappeared.  The new experimental filter retains unrelated
valid derivations and drops the affected descendants.  This expands reuse under
edits without assuming semantic equivalence of the old and new formulas.

First the old proof is independently checked.  Canonical original clauses are
mapped to identical target clauses or marked absent.  In derivation order, a
record survives exactly when its starting clause and every recorded resolution
parent have a mapping.  Surviving references receive new indices; rejected
records receive an absent marker.  This scans the recorded graph without
constructing transitive support sets.  Canonicalization, old-proof checking,
edge inspection, copying and target rechecking are charged.  A separate audit
uses explicit transitive premise sets to check the filter's decisions.

\begin{proposition}[Selective derivation survival and expansion composition]
Fix an acyclic sequence of checked resolution derivations.  Keep each original
premise that appears identically in a target CNF, and retain each derived record
if all of its recorded parents have been retained.  Then a record is retained
if and only if every original premise reachable along its recorded derivation
paths survives.  Every retained clause is implied by the target.  Conjoining
these clauses with the target preserves exactly its models.  A resolution proof
from this augmented formula can be converted to a target proof by substituting
the retained derivations for its learned-clause premises.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
For an original clause the claim is its membership test.  For a derived record,
its reachable original premises are the union of those of its starting clause
and its recorded resolution parents.  Induction in the acyclic order shows that
all of this union survives exactly when every parent survives.  The unchanged
resolution rule then derives the identical claimed clause from target premises.
Thus adding retained clauses removes no target model, while keeping all target
clauses gives the reverse inclusion of model sets.

List the retained derivations before the new proof.  Map each augmented original
clause either to its target-clause index or to a preceding retained derivation
with the identical clause.  Remap each newly derived reference in order.  All
parents still precede their use and each resolution step has the same literal
parents and conclusion.  Induction therefore converts any final empty clause
into a target refutation.  A full model is checked directly against the target;
without a model or empty-clause conclusion, the outcome remains unknown.
\end{proof}

Survival is complete only for the supplied derivation paths.  A discarded clause
may still be a consequence of the edited source via an alternative proof that
has not been found.  No semantic completeness or minimal-support claim follows.
Filtering and substitution are polynomial in the supplied sources and proof
lengths, not necessarily in the target input length alone.  Acquiring and
storing that prior knowledge remain additional obligations.
Old SAT authority is never transported.  A surviving empty clause, including an
empty target clause, can certify UNSAT without expansion; other retained
knowledge remains unknown until further checking establishes an answer.

For expansion, the unchanged learner receives the target plus retained clauses.
Its complete initial proposal is computed from the target alone in both modes.
Additional clauses can still change ordering, propagation and conflict activity.
The final certificate substitutes all reused premises back into target-derived
records and is accepted by the unchanged checker.  Cold replay needs only the
target and that certificate, not the original source, its proof or the transport
procedure.  All retained and newly acquired derivations are persisted, even for
SAT and unknown outcomes; proof pruning and active-index optimization remain
separate questions.

SELECTIVE_MEASUREMENTS

All 2,640 full and 22 focused tests pass.  Exhaustive and adversarial small-case
validation checks 13,624 edited targets from all 512 two-variable clause sets,
3,304 three-variable clause sets of at most three clauses, and 128 fresh sparse
signed mixtures.  Every single canonical-clause deletion is tried for the
two-variable parents; the other parents receive a fixed deletion.  Added
clauses and empty targets are included.  Independent support unions agree with
all survival decisions.  Truth tables verify 10,955 SAT and 2,669 UNSAT composed
answers, and 1,270 retained-clause checks include 1,715 satisfying extensions.
These are cumulative checks, not distinct models or independent problem draws.
At zero expansion budget, 2,329 retained refutations remain certified while
11,295 other results remain unknown.

Focused tests include independent branches, invalid removed derivations,
transitive invalidation, absent source clauses, target additions, empty sources,
tautologies, changed clause indices, partial budgets and proof substitution.
Filtering and certificate checking are exercised with search disabled.
Validation preserves 250 prior manifests, 84,291 artifact entries, all 1,362
prior scientific files and all 89 earlier proof blocks.  Five new scientific
files bring the ledger to 1,367; earlier completed panels and cumulative tables
remain separately denominated and unchanged.

The remaining coverage obligation is new external transfer with a frozen policy.
For edited inputs, further reuse requires finding alternative supports for
rejected consequences and selecting retained knowledge economically.  Retaining
more valid constraints can enlarge the propagation index and final proof enough
to outweigh saved search.  Compact source guards, preprocessing and unrestricted
expansion remain open; finite target coverage is not an efficient classification
of arbitrary CNF.
